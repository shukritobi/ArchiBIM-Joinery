Scale & 2D Documentation

Use SketchUp's native Scale tool, then select the cabinet and click Refresh. The body is rebuilt at the new width/depth/height while keeping panel material thickness unchanged. Mirroring and shear are not supported.

Opening symbols are placed on the ABJ | 2D Elevation Openings tag (off by default for clean 3D views). Turn it on in a front orthographic SketchUp scene or LayOut viewport; dashed opening marks are always included in DXF exports.

ArchiBIM Wall Hosting

To bind: Ctrl-select one generated cabinet AND one ArchiBIM wall in SketchUp, choose exposed face, then Bind. The cabinet snaps its back to the wall's physical face. Auto-sync executes after a completed SketchUp operation, not during dragging.
