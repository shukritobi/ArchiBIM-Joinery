Island, Corner & Cabinet Path

Selected Lines accepts native SketchUp edges on one horizontal plane, including connected 90° runs. Non-orthogonal turns and closed loops are rejected instead of creating bad joins. Corner modules have two perpendicular fillers, initially 50 mm. Island worktop mitres are true 45° profiles; validate fabrication and supports before manufacturing.

Doors, Handles & Appliance Units

Appliance dimensions are placeholders, not appliance installation approvals. Check exact model's cutout, airflow, power and door swing instructions. Tall and wardrobe doors can use a middle groove; overhead wall units do not automatically get a top shadow gap.

Scale & 2D Documentation

Finish SketchUp's native Scale tool, then select the OUTER cabinet group and click Refresh. The body is rebuilt at the new width/depth/height while keeping panel material thickness unchanged. Mirroring and shear are not supported.

Opening symbols are placed on the ABJ | 2D Elevation Openings tag (off by default for clean 3D views). Turn it on in a front orthographic SketchUp scene or LayOut viewport; dashed opening marks are always included in DXF exports.

ArchiBIM Wall Hosting

To bind: Ctrl-select one generated cabinet AND one ArchiBIM wall in SketchUp, choose exposed face, then Bind. The cabinet snaps its back to the wall's physical face. Auto-sync executes after a completed SketchUp operation, not during dragging.
